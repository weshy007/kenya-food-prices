# Pamoja Agri-Advisory: maize market note

Data source: World Food Programme (WFP) Kenya food prices and Humanitarian Data Exchange (HDX) market data.

## Advice for farmers

1. Sell early if you need cash soon after harvest. The cheapest month for wholesale maize in 2015-2019 was month 1, when the average bag price was KES 2594.64; the highest month was 5 at KES 3259.34. This makes January a stronger cash-sale month than the late-spring peak.
2. Holding maize until June can work, but only if storage costs stay low. In 64% of market-years, June prices were higher than January, and the median gain was KES 550.00 per bag. That is a real upside, but it is not guaranteed.
3. Price differences between markets matter a lot. The average retail maize price ranged from KES 36.19/kg to KES 75.84/kg, a gap of KES 39.65/kg. For households, the difference between the cheapest and most expensive markets can be material.

## Advice for county officers

4. Monitor the markets that repeatedly rank as the most expensive. The top retail markets in 2015-2019 were Lodwar and Mandera; both were in the top three every year in the five-year comparison. Those repeated high-price markets are the clearest places to check for transport bottlenecks, supply shortages, or thin trading conditions.

## Bottom line

The historical pattern suggests that maize storage can sometimes pay, but only after covering storage, transport, spoilage, financing, and quality-loss costs. County officers should pay closest attention to markets that remain persistently expensive rather than assuming a single county-wide trend.
